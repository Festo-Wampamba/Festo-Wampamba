# Support card logos

`logos.json` retains the white vector logos from the existing Shields.io PayPal, Ko-fi and Patreon badges. The new local SVG cards use the original badge colors as the icon backgrounds: PayPal `#00457C`, Ko-fi `#F16061`, Patreon `#F96854`.

The coffee cup and repository star are simple native SVG illustrations. The coffee accent remains yellow. All account destinations are preserved from the profile README; generating cards needs no network access.

Regenerate with `pnpm generate:support`.
